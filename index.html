How I test

I follow the same six steps on every target, and I write down what I find as I go.

  1. Recon

    Understand the target and map its attack surface.

  2. Enumeration

    Find services, technologies, endpoints, users, and entry points.

  3. Assessment

    Identify weaknesses and judge their impact.

  4. Exploitation

    Safely confirm each vulnerability in an authorized environment.

  5. Escalation

    Check whether access can be raised, where it applies.

  6. Reporting

    Document the impact and how to fix it.
